Flight-Importer is a personal-use extension to a flight fare monitor. It is designed to organize airline confirmation emails, receipts, cancellations, and changes into accurate reservation records.

What the app does

  • Reads messages and attached original emails from the owner's dedicated import mailbox.
  • Extracts ticket references, passenger details, flight dates and numbers, fare products, and amounts paid in cash or miles.
  • Matches receipts and cancellation notices to the correct tickets, and flags incomplete or ambiguous information for review.
  • Uses confirmed reservation details with the owner's fare monitor to check prices and manage applicable award alerts.

The mailbox connection currently starts in review mode. Automatic reservation changes and receipt reminders require the importer to be enabled after validation.

Google account access

The desktop app requests read-only Gmail access to the dedicated mailbox selected by its owner. The app verifies the expected mailbox address before saving a connection. Its requested permission does not allow it to send, delete, or modify Gmail messages, or access Google Drive or Calendar.

The companion runs on the owner's PC. This public website provides information only; it does not accept receipts, connect mailboxes, or display reservation data.

Read the privacy policy for details about data use, storage, external services, and revoking access.

For personal use

Flight-Importer is operated by its owner for personal travel management. It is not a public booking service, and it does not purchase, cancel, or change tickets with an airline. For questions, use the user-support email shown on its Google authorization screen.
